What APMI does

Four pillars of the mandate

01

Advocacy

Representing member interests directly with SEBI and shaping regulatory consultations that affect the PMS industry.

02

Compliance support

Circulars, manuals, and Compliance Sutra sessions that translate regulation into practical operating guidance.

03

Investor protection

Benchmarking, valuation, and disclosure standards that keep reporting consistent and transparent for clients.

04

Industry standards

Building shared frameworks — from performance validation to cyber resilience — across the member base.
