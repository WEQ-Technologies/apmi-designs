APMI Overview

The institutional foundation and statutory origin of the Association of Portfolio Managers in India.

Constituted under the regulatory guidance of SEBI and incorporated on 31st December 2021 as a Section 8 non-profit entity, APMI is the apex industry body representing SEBI-registered Portfolio Managers nationwide — promoting high ethical standards, standardized reporting, and research-backed best practices to bolster investor confidence.

The APMI Charter

The four foundational pillars defining APMI's core operational responsibilities, regulatory cooperation, and institutional oversight.

PILLAR 01

POLICY FORMULATION

Play a role in helping the regulator and government agencies/market intermediaries evolve operating policies for members conducive to orderly functioning and growth. Guide members on implementation and control mechanisms.

PILLAR 02

INFORMATION DISSEMINATION AND KNOWLEDGE REPOSITORY

Impart knowledge on latest developments via industry experts. Act as a repository for industry data and as the first port of call for new entrants/prospective PMS managers for guidance and know-how.

PILLAR 03

INDUSTRY ADVOCACY

Present a coherent view and perspective of the industry through varied communication activities to the public at large with an aim to instill confidence.

PILLAR 04

ETHICS & GOVERNANCE

Define and maintain high professional and ethical standards in all areas of operation to protect investor interests. Recommend and promote best business practices and code of conduct.

Historical Evolution

Evolution of APMI Timeline

The milestone journey of APMI from its regulatory inception in 2022 through nationwide digital standardization and strategic priorities going forward.

YEAR 2022
Operationalization
  • First Guidance Released (28th May 2022): Covering withdrawal of work-from-home flexibility for critical functions (Investments, Dealing, Operations, Compliance, Risk Management) post-COVID.
  • Formation & Operationalization of Working Committees: Operationalization of APMI's 3 working committees from June 2022 to spearhead specialized industry initiatives.
[View In Detail]
YEAR 2023
Standardization & Reconstitution
  • Flagging of Demat Accounts (May 2023): Implementation & execution of workflow on Flagging of Demat Accounts in May 2023 for enhanced regulatory traceability.
  • Standardized Redemption Timelines (May 2023): Standardized time period for payout of funds/securities to investors during redemption in May 2023 for industry uniformity.
  • Second Reconstitution of APMI Board (30th June 2023): Second reconstitution of APMI Board & Working Committees successfully completed on 30th June 2023.
[View In Detail]
YEAR 2024
100% Digital Transformation
  • Digitalization & Process Streamlining: Organizations are able to streamline all their processes through digitalization, and are also exploring new avenues of growth by adopting 100% digitization—one such major change that has come into the financial services industry is digital client onboarding. Clients onboarding through the digital route is now a well-accepted way which provides scalability, efficiency and much more.
  • SEBI Circular on Digital Client Onboarding (May 2024): Taking the submission of APMI forward, SEBI had released a circular on Digital Client Onboarding in May 2024.
  • Standard Procedure & Fee Illustrations: As mandated by the regulator, APMI has specified a standard procedure for on-boarding of clients through digital mode in consultation with SEBI. This also covers aspects related to calculation of performance fee & creating illustrations for the same.
[View In Detail]
GOING FORWARD
Strategic Horizons
  • PMS as Preferred Choice: Work towards making Portfolio Management Services offered by SEBI Registered Portfolio Managers as a preferred choice for Investors with increasing the reach by educative and marketing efforts.
  • Investor Education Initiatives: Work on nationwide Investor Education to empower HNIs and institutions with clear understanding of risk-adjusted returns and PMS fiduciary mechanisms.
  • Industry-Wide Data Standards: Making the best and digital use of data which can be a source of Industry wide data for comparison and setting industry wide standards.
  • Ease of Doing Business: Completion & Execution of various submissions under the Ease of Doing Business framework to eliminate redundant compliance friction.
